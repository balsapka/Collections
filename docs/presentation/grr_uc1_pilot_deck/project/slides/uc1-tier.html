Leaving UC1 out of the persona and the tier only holds if we are not leaving customers with good UC1 signals out of in-house. We checked. Agency-tier card accounts with a positive UC1 reading pay 13 percent next month, against about 36 percent for in-house accounts; their three-month money sits at the in-house cut, about 1,010 against 1,031 dirhams, not above it; on loans it is below the cut, 4,220 against 4,790. Among loan customers we cannot reach, a positive reading goes with more payment, 26 percent against 16 to 19 percent after other conversations, about 130 accounts a month, but still not at in-house level; on cards the difference is small, 13 against 12 percent. The one exception is the card non-workable pool: accounts there with a recent positive UC1 reading pay 8.6 percent, against 2 to 3 percent after other conversations, which is as much as the agency tier pays; that is about 115 card accounts a month. So UC1 can stay out of the in-house selection, and the exception is worth tracking. These are associations on UC1's August refresh, not effects; the loan numbers are small, and the tier figures come from the latest persona run. We confirm them once UC1 has read all calls.
