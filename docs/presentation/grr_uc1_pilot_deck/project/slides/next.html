To start on 4 January we need four decisions from GRR and two pieces of data, by 11 December. The decisions: which fields the card shows in DCORE, and whether it also shows the signals behind each state or UC1's planned collection action; the card test, meaning half or 20 percent of accounts on the card, the measure and the bar; the tier, whether to show it, test it or leave it for later, and the cut-offs; and the DCORE load, its format and monthly date, plus the segment report. The data: from DCORE, each account's placement and the discounts and waivers given; and the cost per call day and the agency rate card, or a proxy agreed with finance. The timeline: agree all this by 11 December, when the 30 November month end is scored, with UC1's delinquency reason and last call on the card; UC1's other fields follow once UC1 has read the remaining calls; selection and a dry run of the DCORE load from 11 to 20 December; the card goes live on 4 January; monthly readouts from February and the first full readout in late April.
