What UC1's readings tell us so far, on UC1's August refresh and the about 1 in 5 accounts with a reading; still early. The delinquency reason separates who pays: customers who still have a job pay more than those who lost it, on cards 17 against 11 percent, on loans 27 against 20 percent, and the gap is larger when the call is recent, about 9 points on cards. That is useful context for the collector. UC1 fits beside the persona: the persona ranks customers, and UC1 adds why they fell behind and what they said, with the call date; the tier slide shows that keeping UC1 out of the tier leaves no in-house-level customers out. Stated ability on cards is shown as context; on its own it does not order payers where ability is Unknown. UC1 reads per customer, not per product, so a reading shows on each of the customer's accounts; about 18 percent of card accounts belong to customers with another product overdue. At 180+, customers with a recorded call pay 5 to 14 percent against about 1 percent, so those calls are worth reading; UC1 holds calls for under 1 percent of 180+ customers so far. Stated intent where no promise is on record, and disputes, we look at next, once UC1 has read all calls.
