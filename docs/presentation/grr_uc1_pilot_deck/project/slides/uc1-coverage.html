All figures are 61 to 180 accounts. The first row is the upper bound: accounts with a DCORE result that records a conversation, or a call transcript. That is 46 percent of card and 51 percent of loan accounts any time from March to August, and 26 and 31 percent in the 90 days before the July month end. We count conversations, not every right-party entry: only about 30 percent of right-party days are a conversation, and most of the rest are payment postings and received e-mails. Results we could not classify are included; without them the bound is about 5 points lower. About a third of accounts have a Genesys call transcript, and 22 and 20 percent have a UC1 reading, from a call or an agent remark: about 1 in 5 accounts, about half of the card customers and 2 in 5 of the loan customers who talked to us. These are collections calls, all outbound. Transcripts reach a customer through the account on the call; 28 percent of Genesys calls carry no account link, so the transcript and UC1 rows are a floor. On the right, these customers' calls from March to August: UC1 has processed about 1 in 8 of the card calls and 1 in 4 of the loan calls. That leaves about 30 thousand card and 5 thousand loan calls to process, plus about 2,400 card and 550 loan calls since September, which come through Genesys until the end of September and through PCA from October.
