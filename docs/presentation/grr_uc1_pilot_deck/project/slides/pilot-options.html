For the pilot we see three building blocks. The base is the card test: a random group of customers sees the card in DCORE and the rest are worked as today, so we can measure what the card adds; with about 7,500 card accounts per group. The other two are options for GRR, both about the tier. The tier test only touches accounts whose tier differs from today's placement: for a random part of them we follow the tier, so agency-tier accounts worked in-house go to an agency and in-house-tier accounts at an agency come back; the rest stay as they are. It shows whether the money collected, net of agency commission or in-house cost, holds up; about 2,300 accounts per group are enough to rule out a drop of 3 percentage points from the agency tier's 17 percent payment rate. The 180+ option is the same idea in reverse: the 12 percent of 180+ accounts with a positive signal, where about one in five pays against under 3 percent of the rest, and a random half of them gets an in-house attempt; about 1,100 per group can detect a rise of 5 points. If GRR prefers not to test tiers yet, the tier simply stays a field on the card and in the segment report.
