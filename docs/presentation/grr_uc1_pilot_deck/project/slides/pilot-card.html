How the card test works. Every 61 to 180 account our own teams work goes, at random and by customer, into one of two groups, balanced within product, tier and DPD bucket. For the card group DCORE shows the card; for the other group the card stays empty and the accounts are worked exactly as today. The same collectors work both groups, they decide what to do in both, and a customer keeps their group for the whole pilot. The primary measure is the average money collected per account in the three months after the month end, net of discounts and waivers, compared between the card group and the group without it: the difference is what the card adds. With about 7,500 card accounts per group, a difference of about 2 percentage points in the share of accounts that pay would show. If collections prefers a smaller start, a random 20 percent on the card still shows about 3 points. Among accounts with a recent UC1 reading, about 530 card accounts per group at today's coverage of about 1 in 14, about 10 points would show; loans only as direction. Collectors may carry some of what they learn over to accounts without the card, so the result is a lower bound. Readouts are monthly from February, with the first full readout in late April.
