UC1 · how it joins

Three ways UC1 could join the persona: beside it fits the data best

Proposed

A · Beside the persona

What it means

UC1’s fields sit on the card next to the four states. The persona and the tier stay as they are.

What we found

A customer with no call loses nothing; the reason and last call add context.

Not proposed

B · A fifth axis inside the persona

What it means

UC1’s states become part of the persona itself.

What we found

Four in five accounts would sit in “no reading”, and every state multiplies the persona count.

Tested, dropped

C · Feed UC1 into the four axes

What it means

For example, promises made on calls count toward credibility.

What we found

It changes at most 4 in 1,000 accounts: UC1 adds more beside the persona than inside it.

There are three ways UC1 could join the personas. A: beside the persona, as fields on the card next to the four states, with the persona and the tier unchanged. B: as a fifth axis inside the persona. C: feeding what customers say into the four axes, for example counting promises made on calls toward credibility. We propose A. It fits the data: most customers will never have a UC1 reading, so every account must get its persona without one, and under A a customer with no call loses nothing, while the delinquency reason and the last call give the collector context. B would put four in five accounts into a no-reading state, more than nine in ten without a recent call, and multiply the persona count again. C we tested: adding call promises to credibility changes at most 4 in 1,000 accounts, so UC1 adds more beside the persona than inside it. The ground rules for A: UC1's own estimate is the value we show, built from call transcripts and agent remarks and always shown with its call date, because recent calls tell more; it never changes a persona state; and each signal is kept or dropped once UC1 has read all available calls.
