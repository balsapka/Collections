This is what we hand over from January: a collector card for every account, refreshed after every month end and loaded into DCORE, so collectors and the strategy team see it next to the account. The top row is the persona from what the customer does: deterioration, locatability, credibility and ability. The dashed blue box is UC1, what the customer said on calls: when they last spoke to us, whether they say they can pay, why they fell behind, what they intend, over what period, whether they named an amount, a date or a plan, and any dispute. The card describes the customer; it has no score and no suggested action, and what collectors do with it stays GRR's decision. Unknown means we hold no data, not a bad sign: ability is unknown for more than half of card accounts. The UC1 fields stay empty until UC1 has read a call or an agent remark for that customer, about 1 in 5 card and loan accounts today but only 1 in 14 from a call in the last 90 days, and each value comes with its call date, because a customer's situation changes. UC1 reads per customer, not per product: a customer with two overdue products shows the same reading on both, as for about 18 percent of card accounts. The tier is dashed because it is our one proposal and shows only if GRR wants it. Alongside the card, GRR gets a monthly segment report.
