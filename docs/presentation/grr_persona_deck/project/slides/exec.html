Three messages. One: beyond 60 DPD there is no model; cases are allocated by bucket and product, and the late buckets are where data runs dry. Two: we built synthesized features, signals from every data domain condensed into persona states that answer three questions in order. Three: what it shows. Tested on unseen months, the top fifth of card accounts ranked by persona hold 54 percent of the money collected, against 30 percent when ranked by DPD bucket. Card customers who are curing now pay within three months 60 percent of the time, against 22 percent for those who cured before. At 180+, 12 percent of accounts show a positive signal, and about one in five of them pays, against under 3 percent of the rest. Next: finalise the axes with GRR, including whether ability ships now or after the bureau feed; integrate UC1 into the final personas; a workshop on collection strategy and how GRR uses the output; and for the risk score, test whether a full Retail 360 model adds more than the personas, then decide whether it ships with them or in Phase 2.
