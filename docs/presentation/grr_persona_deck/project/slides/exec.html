Pilot

Three messages. One: beyond 60 DPD there is no model; cases are allocated by bucket and product, and the late buckets are where data runs dry. Two: the UC2 synthesized features take signals from every data domain and condense them each month into four answers per account: how did they get here, can we reach them, will they pay, and can they pay. Three: what it shows. Ranked on these four answers, the top fifth of card accounts brought in 54 percent of the money collected in the next three months. Within the same DPD bucket, 91 to 120, groups of loan customers pay anywhere from 9 to 76 percent of the time within three months, depending on their answers: about eight times apart. At 180+, 12 percent of accounts show a positive signal, and about one in five of them pays, against under 3 percent of the rest. Next: by 20 November the UC2 synthesized features are signed off; one, ability, may follow later, pending bureau data and the framework. From 20 November to 11 December, the UC1 transcript signals and UC2 are combined, with action support. Pilot selection from 11 to 20 December, and the pilot from 4 January.
