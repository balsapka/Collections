Pilot

Three messages. One: beyond 60 DPD there is no model; cases are allocated by bucket and product, and the late buckets are where data runs dry. Two: synthesized features take signals from every data domain and condense them each month into four answers per account: how did they get here, can we reach them, will they pay, and can they pay. Three: what it shows. The top fifth of card accounts ranked by persona brought in 54 percent of the money collected in the next three months. For loans, 74 percent of customers who are curing now made a meaningful payment within three months, against 38 percent of those who cured before. At 180+, 12 percent of accounts show a positive signal, and about one in five of them pays, against under 3 percent of the rest. Next: by 20 November, deterioration, locatability and credibility are complete with today's feedback in; ability waits on bureau data and a workshop on the new framework. From 20 November to 11 December, the UC1 signals and the UC2 axes come together into one framework with action support, with credibility and ability ready to take the UC1 signals. Pilot selection from 11 to 20 December, and the pilot from 4 January.
