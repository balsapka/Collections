Cards 61–180 · August 2026 · credibility High / Moderate / Low; ability includes Unknown

Part 2 starts by putting the axes together. A persona is one combination of states: one of six deterioration states, one of nine locatability cells, one of three credibility bands and one of four ability bands. That is 648 possible personas before anything from UC1. The transcript signals, such as stated intent, a hardship reason or a dispute, come on top, and each one multiplies the count again. Many of these combinations do occur, and most hold only a handful of accounts. Nobody can write and run a separate strategy for hundreds of personas, so we need a way to decide where a strategy is actually needed.
