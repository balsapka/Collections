10b

An alternative to the scoring on the previous slide, for discussion. The only change is how promises score: any promise in the last twelve months earns a point, even a broken one or one still open, and keeping it earns more: three for kept, two for partly kept, one otherwise; no promise stays at zero. Paying while delinquent, the record before, the bands and the refusal rule are unchanged. The reason: a customer who made a promise has engaged with repaying. Compared at the same bucket, payment record and reachability, card customers who broke a promise paid more often than customers we reached who never promised, by about six points. Because every promise gains a point and the bands stay the same, rows only move up: at 61 to 180, High rises from about four to twelve percent and Low falls from about three quarters to just over half. The customers who move up pay between the bands they leave and join, and High plus Moderate now hold 78 percent of the money collected from cards, against 53 percent. The better ranking holds in every month and within every DPD band.
