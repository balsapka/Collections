What to expect

What the framework can and cannot do today

It can

  • Describe every 60+ account monthly, cards and loans
  • Explain each state with a rule and a reason code
  • Rank cure and roll on top of the DPD band
  • Show where the data gaps are, and their size

Expect

  • Many Unknowns at 180+ (about 9 in 10 on ability)
  • High states stay rare until income data improves
  • States that describe, not causes; treatments need tests
  • Thresholds tuned with GRR as evidence comes in

Coverage roadmap: bureau salary feed (ability) · day-level loan repayments (credibility) · VPN check on foreign logins (locatability) · 180+ back-tests.

180+ ability Unknown: cards 87.4%, loans 89.5%, 31 Aug 2026

Setting expectations honestly is part of the pitch. The framework already covers every account and explains every state. It will show a lot of Unknown at 180+, because those customers have largely left the bank’s ecosystem; that is information, not failure. High states will stay rare until income data improves. States describe; they do not prove cause, so treatments need champion-challenger tests. The coverage roadmap names the specific data each axis needs next.
