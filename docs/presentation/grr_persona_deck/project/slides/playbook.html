Gone dark → skip tracing and new contact data, not more dialing

Deceased → claims and insurance route, stop contact

Hypotheses, not policy: each is tested champion vs challenger before it changes a strategy

This is where the framework becomes strategy, and these are hypotheses for GRR to own, not decisions. The persona says what to offer; the contact route says through which channel; the journey adds context, for example never-settled customers feed back to underwriting and sudden stops point to a life event and hardship options. The biggest immediate lever is the contact route: customers who have gone dark should get skip tracing rather than more dial attempts, and customers abroad need different channels. Every row should be tested champion versus challenger before it changes policy.
