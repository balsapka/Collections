The transcript work, UC1, joins in the nuance step, next to credibility and ability. It only applies where a conversation took place, and that differs sharply across the illustrative tiers: 89 percent of in-house card accounts had a right-party contact in the last three months, 45 percent of the agency group and 16 percent of the non-workable pool. So transcripts will add most where we already talk to customers. They add what the data cannot: what the customer said about intent, income, hardship, disputes and promises.
