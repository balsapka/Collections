Cards 61–180 · paid = meaningful payment within 3 months (back-test)

The same book, split in the order a case meets the framework: first how they got here, then whether we can talk to them, then, for the reachable, whether they show credibility or the means to pay. Each end point shows its share of card accounts and how often it made a meaningful payment in the following three months. Customers curing now pay most whatever else is true: 68 percent when reachable and credible or able, still 44 percent when we cannot reach them. At the other end, those who stopped suddenly and cannot be reached are half the card book and pay 8 percent; never-paid customers we cannot reach pay 3 percent. Reachability splits every family. This is an illustrative simplification, not a proposal: it merges personas, ignoring location and combining credibility and ability bands. How personas are grouped for strategy is for the GRR workshop.
