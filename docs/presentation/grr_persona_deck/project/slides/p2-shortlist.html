Where this leads. Most personas fall to the agency or the non-workable pool and need nothing more from us. The in-house tier is a much shorter list: 51 personas on cards, and 26 of them cover 80 percent of in-house accounts; on loans 11 of 16. Look at the five largest: every one is reachable, while credibility and ability vary, from low credibility with unknown ability to moderate credibility with low ability. Reachability decides who lands in-house; the strategy question is what to do within it. That short list is what we would take to GRR: the data proposes it, UC1 refines it with what customers tell us, and GRR decides the offer, agent and channel for each persona in the strategy workshop. The simplification comes from GRR, not from us merging states.
