Where this leads. Most personas fall to the agency or the non-workable pool and need nothing more from us. The ones that land in-house are a much shorter list. On cards, a third of in-house accounts are customers curing now who are credible or able, then those who cured before and those who stopped suddenly, again credible or able. A small number of personas cover most in-house accounts. That short list is what we would take to GRR: the data proposes it, UC1 refines it with what customers tell us, and GRR decides the offer, agent and channel for each persona in the strategy workshop. The simplification comes from GRR's judgement, not from us merging states.
