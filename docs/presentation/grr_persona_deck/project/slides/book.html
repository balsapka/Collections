Production, 31 Aug 2026, cards 61–180 (19,292); 82%: promise dimension Thin, back-test base 31 May 2026

Four messages for strategy that hold even before the combined back-test. One: two thirds of the 61–180 card book failed the last three call days, and most accounts get six or more call days a quarter, so dialer capacity is concentrated on customers who do not answer. Two: 44% were last seen abroad, rising since June. Three: for 57% we cannot see capacity, mostly because they bank elsewhere; the bureau salary feed is the planned fix. Four: few promises reach a conclusion, so the promise record is thin for most customers. Together: invest in contact data, international channels and income data.
