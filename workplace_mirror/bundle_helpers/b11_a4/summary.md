**What this bundle does.** RL A4 no longer uses the ECL gap rule, now that the history source covers all loans, and a matched restructured loan now sees the history of its original loans.

- **Removed**: `ecl_gap_blind_spot_flag` and the rule behind it (`assign_state`, `classify`, `build_dpd_sequences`, `build_deterioration`), the parameter `rl_ecl_gap_cutoff_date`, and `disbursement_date` / `product_code` in `crx_rl_full_history`, which only that rule read.
- **Why**: the rule looked at disbursement date and product code alone, never at the history. Those accounts went to Stabilized or Unknown even with a full clean history.
- **Restructure link** (`rl_a4_deterioration/chains.py`, node `build_rl_full_history_linked`): reads `int_loan_restructure_link`, walks chains transitively (cycles end), puts the original loans' non-closed months in front of the account's own (worst bucket per month, own row wins an overlap) and writes `crx_rl_full_history_linked`, `crx_rl_restructure_chains`, `crx_rl_dpd_sequences_linked`. `crx_rl_a4_deterioration` gains `chain_known_date`, `chain_n_originals`, `chain_match_type`, `chain_candidate_count`, `chain_months_added`, `chain_stitched_flag`.
- **No leakage**: an observation before the later of the restructure flag and the closure reads the account's own history. That needed one small engine change: `build_deterioration` has `log_states`, and the wrapper classifies the two groups and logs their union.
- **Decided by the user**: a reset to 0 DPD after a restructure stays in the history as an ended spell (a re-default within 12 months is `repeat_delinquent`); ambiguous links (`candidate_count > 1`) are stitched and counted; unmatched restructured loans stay blind for life, and so does a chain ending in an unmatched restructure. `orig_loan_peak_dpd` / `orig_loan_dpd_pre_close` are not used.
- **Unchanged**: `ECL_CUTOFF_DATE` and the source union in `stg_rr_portf.py`, `crx_rl_spell_table`, `rl_a1`, every CC result, every state rule.
- **Check** (`rl_a4_deterioration/b11_check.py`, pipeline `crx_rl_a4_deterioration_b11_check`): the new state goes to temporary entries and `crx_rl_a4_deterioration` stays untouched as the baseline until step 8. Blocks `B11 OUTPUT` (old × new, new by `loan_type`), `ECL` (flagged accounts: does their history now start before 2025-12, and what they became), `CHAIN` (stitched or still blind by `loan_type`, match type, ambiguous links, months added, old × new), `UNKNOWN` (what is left, by route × `loan_type`).
- **Expected**: Unknown falls and, after this bundle, only restructured and cropped accounts can be Unknown, so route `other` must be 0. In `B11 ECL`, `2025_12_or_later` should be near 0: those accounts still miss their older history and would now get a state from a short history. The `is_restructured` flag is sparse, so the link's recall is unknown; `B11 CHAIN` shows how many restructured accounts still stay blind.
