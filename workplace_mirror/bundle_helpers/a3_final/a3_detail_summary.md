## Observations

1. **Order:** High > Moderate > Low holds on paid_due and paid_any in every model, every split (loan type, charge-off
   age, DPD bucket) and every outcome month; the only breaks are cured / rolled at 61-180 (RL cured is lowest for High in
   every split; CC breaks are <= 0.5 pp or masked by exited). State AUC on paid_due is 0.65-0.74 overall, weakest at CC
   181-360 (0.576) and RL 151-180 (0.608).
2. **Stability:** states are sticky: 88-90% unchanged month to month at 61-180 and 99.3-99.5% at 180+, two-step moves <=
   0.2%; High is the least sticky (72-82% stay, nearly all the rest drop to Moderate). The mix itself drifts at 61-180 as
   the population grows (cc_60_180 High 4.1% -> 2.5%, Low 72.7% -> 78.0%, rows 14.7k -> 19.3k).
3. **Overlap with other axes:** most with A4 at 61-180 (V 0.41 CC, 0.36 RL; 73% of CC High are "Stabilized"); at 180+
   with A2 reachability (RL 0.235) and A4 (CC 0.22); A1 overlaps least at 61-180 (0.06-0.11). All V <= 0.41, so A3 adds
   information the other axes do not carry.
4. **Points at 180+:** 0 points pay almost nothing (paid_due 0.7% CC, 0.5% RL), 1 point 9.5% / 4.5%, then a step to
   27.7% / 24.5% at 2, 42.8% / 54.9% at 3 and 49.5% / 66.6% at 4; 5 has too few rows. That supports Moderate from 2; High
   (4+) is tiny (CC 161, RL 109 rows in Aug) and 4 adds only 7 pp (CC) over 3.

   charge-off), so rolled / cured are not readable there and rolled falls with DPD bucket mechanically; (b) -1 points
