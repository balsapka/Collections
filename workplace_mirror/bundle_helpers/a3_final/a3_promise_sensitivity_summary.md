## Verdicts

1. **Kept-rate floor (S1): no floor holds in all four model_ids.** At 60-180 the rows below a 40% floor pay like Partly
   kept, not Promised (cc 33.8 vs 34.3 Partly kept vs 23.5 Promised; rl 53.9 vs 52.8 vs 44.3), so a floor costs AUC
   (R1_40: cc -0.0050 [-0.0062, -0.0036], rl -0.0030 [-0.0051, -0.0006]). At 180+ they do pay like Promised from about
   30-50% (cc 16.8 vs 16.8; rl 22.0 vs 19.8), but the AUC change is nil (cc -0.0002, rl -0.0000) because those rows are
   under 4% of the base. The under-20% bins are too thin to read (at most 105 rows per model).
2. **Within B level (S1.2): no floor either.** Once B is fixed the rate bins sit within a few points of Promised and do
   not order (cc 180+ in B some: 20-<40% -8.6, 40-<60% +0.4, 60-<80% +0.5, 80-100% +8.6); only the top bin separates, in
   B some and none at 180+. Most of the raw gradient by kept rate is B. Within B, R0 Partly kept minus Promised is -0.8
   / +5.0 / +1.1 pp (cc 60-180, regular / some / none) and No promise sits 5 to 15 pp below Promised in B some and none
   (regular: level mix too thin to separate), so Dimension A's value is mainly "any event versus none".
3. **Recency (S2.2): adds beyond rate and B at 180+, not at 60-180, and as a rule it adds no AUC.** Raw gap last event
   kept vs not kept: cc 60-180 38.6 vs 30.5, cc 180+ 36.3 vs 17.8, rl 180+ 43.4 vs 23.2, rl 60-180 52.8 vs 53.4. Within
   B at 60-180 the gap vanishes or reverses (cc some 34.3 vs 34.8, cc none 10.9 vs 15.7, rl some 45.8 vs 54.1); at 180+
   it holds in every B level (cc some 33.3 vs 18.6, cc none 18.2 vs 5.7, rl some 29.5 vs 19.5). Rules R2 / R3: AUC
   -0.0001 to -0.0078 at 60-180 (the moved rows pay 30-35% in cc, above Promised) and 0.0000 to -0.0003 at 180+. Days
   since the last kept event is a steep gradient (cc 60-180 64.2 to 27.2, cc 180+ 42.5 / 60.5 / 46.3 / 19.8) but was not
   tested within B and is not a candidate rule here.
4. **Simplest rule that holds the level order everywhere and costs no AUC (S3, S4): none qualifies as a change.** Level
   order is not a clean test: R0 itself holds in 9 of 14 band units (thin Kept cells), all rows 4 of 4. R1_40 and above,
   R2, R3_2, R4 and R4b lose AUC in at least 1 of 4 model_ids (R1_40 2/4, R2 2/4, R3_2 3/4, R4 2/4 pass); R1_20 / R1_30
   pass but move at most 1,408 rows at 60-180; R3_3 is AUC-neutral (4/4, 13/14 bands) but gains nothing and keeps order
   in 9 of 14 bands. Recommendation: keep R0's levels; no floor, no recency rule.
5. **Kept rule (R5): 70% / 90% / last-2-kept change nothing (AUC within +-0.0004); "at least 3 concluded" does.** Kept
   on exactly 2 concluded events pays like Partly kept (cc 60-180 36.8 vs 34.3, rl 60-180 50.5 vs 52.8, cc 180+ 33.7 vs
   31.6, rl 180+ 41.4 vs 39.4), while Kept on 3+ pays 51.1 / 60.1 / 56.9 / 42.2. R5_min3: AUC +0.0019 [+0.0014, +0.0023]
   cc 60-180 (+0.0018 to +0.0021 in every band), +0.0005 [-0.0005, +0.0016] rl 60-180, 0.0000 at 180+; level order 13 of
   14 bands, AUC 14 of 14. The price is a small Kept level (1.5% of rows at cc 60-180, 45 rows at rl 180+). The 80% rate
   is not contradicted.
6. **Count check: 0 mismatches** for concluded, kept, events in the window, Dimension A level and state, in all four
   model_ids (74,256 / 12,512 / 1,544,188 / 266,430 account-months).
