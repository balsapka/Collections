Yes, partly a significance question, but I can't trust a test on the current sample for two reasons:

1. It's not random. Only part of the calls are extracted so far (about 16% card / 35% loan, up to 16 July), so the customers we see are a selection. A p-value assumes random sampling, and more of a skewed sample doesn't fix the skew.
2. The decision is made inside persona groups, not on the whole book (e.g. does stated ability separate payers where we can't otherwise judge ability?). At ~5% card / 8% loan coverage, each group has a few dozen customers, and results flip between months: one loan signal was strong over April–July and gone in July alone.

Joining to the full history helped the interim run, but personas are scored at a month end, so the signal has to be checked on that month's customers.

So I already have interim proposals for the workshop; the full run is what lets me sign them off per group and see how much of the scored book a signal reaches.
