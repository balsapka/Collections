## 2. Deletion order (one bundle, four ordered steps)

Why order matters: `rl_departure_signature/nodes.py:42` imports `_TRAVEL_KEYWORDS_RE` and
`_TRAVEL_MCCS` from `cc_departure_signature.nodes` at module level, and
`rl_a4_spine/pipeline.py:17` (tier K) imports `build_rl_depart_spine` from
`rl_departure_signature.nodes`. `crx_rl_a4_spine` feeds `crx_rl_a3_payment_credibility`. So:

1. **Step A, move** `build_rl_depart_spine` into `rl_a4_spine/nodes.py` and repoint
   `rl_a4_spine/pipeline.py`. After this step nothing live imports `rl_departure_signature`.
2. **Step B, RL departure**: delete `rl_departure_signature/`, its registry import and entry,
   its three catalog entries. It must go before or with `cc_departure_signature/` because it
   imports it.
3. **Step C, CC D packages**: delete `cc_utilisation/` (reads `crx_cc_payment_trajectory`),
   `cc_payment_trajectory/`, `cc_departure_signature/` (reads `crx_cc_payment_trajectory`), with
   their registry imports and entries and their 17 catalog entries. The three share no Python
   imports, so the inner order is a data-flow courtesy only.
4. **Step D, old A2**: delete `cc_a2_locatability/nodes.py` and `constants.py`, and
   `rl_a2_locatability/nodes.py`. `rl_a2_locatability/nodes.py` imports
   `cc_a2_locatability.nodes`, and nothing imports either (the wrappers import
   `a2_locatability.pipeline`). Independent of A to C, so it can go first or last.
5. **Step E, tier L unused symbols**. Independent of the rest.

Steps A to C are one commit if the bundle ships once. They must not be split across bundles
in the order B, A (RL A3 breaks), nor C before B (import error at registry import).
Bundle workflow: deletions need `apply_bundle.py` to delete directories; the cleanup prompt
removes the HDFS folders (section 6).
